# Store listing (English)

## Google Play

**App name** (max. 30 characters)

    Money Tracker – Expenses

**Short description** (max. 80 characters)

    Track income and expenses – offline, no ads, no account.

**Full description** (max. 4000 characters)

    Money Tracker is a private budget book for your income and expenses. All data stays on your device: no internet connection, no ads, no tracking, no account.

    THE BIG PICTURE IN SECONDS
    • Balance, income, expenses and savings rate of the month at a glance
    • Projection to the end of the month and average per day
    • Upcoming recurring payments and open debts right on the home screen

    RECORD TRANSACTIONS QUICKLY
    • Income and expenses with categories and sub categories
    • Built-in calculator in the amount field (e.g. 12.50+4)
    • Notes, places, people, events and receipt photos
    • Transfers between wallets, with fees and different currencies
    • Templates for frequent transactions

    STATISTICS THAT HELP
    • Spending by category as a donut chart with drill down
    • Comparison with last month and the 3-month average
    • 12-month trend, daily spending, largest expenses
    • Calendar with a spending heat map and full text search with filters
    • Custom first day of the month, e.g. your payday

    WALLETS, DEBTS AND RECURRING PAYMENTS
    • Any number of wallets in any currency
    • Debts and credits with repayments and due dates
    • Recurring transactions: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly

    YOUR DATA IS YOURS
    • Works completely offline – the app has no internet permission
    • PIN lock with fingerprint
    • Backups, optionally protected with an AES-256 password, automatic or manual
    • Export to CSV, Excel (XLSX) and PDF
    • Compatible with MoneyWallet backups and CSV files – move in either direction

    Money Tracker is free software (GPL v3).
